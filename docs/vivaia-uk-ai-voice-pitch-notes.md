# VIVAIA UK AI voice pitch notes
_Date: 2026-05-10_

## Recommended structure
Yes, move **WhatsApp** from Phase 1 to **Phase 2**.

That makes the opening offer cleaner, lower-risk, and easier for VIVAIA to approve.

## Phase 1
### AI voice concierge + CRM intelligence layer
Goal: ensure every London store enquiry is answered, captured, tagged, and analyzed.

### What Phase 1 includes
- AI voice for London store inbound enquiries
- approved answers for common store and product questions
- escalation to human staff when needed
- CRM capture for every interaction
- automatic tagging of the reason for the call
- dashboard / reporting on enquiry patterns

### What the CRM should capture
- caller name
- phone number
- date and time
- reason for enquiry
- product category interest
- specific product/style mentioned
- fit issue or pain point
- store visit intent
- whether follow-up is needed
- outcome of call
- whether escalated

### Recommended reason-for-call tags
- store hours / location
- directions / parking
- stock availability
- size / fit question
- wide feet / comfort question
- returns / exchanges
- care / washing
- styling help
- new arrivals
- restock request
- appointment / assisted shopping
- complaint / escalation

### What Phase 1 gives VIVAIA
- better customer experience
- fewer missed opportunities
- structured first-party customer data
- clear insight into what customers ask most
- visibility into store demand themes
- better decisions for staffing, product focus, and future follow-up

### Best way to describe Phase 1
We make sure every London store enquiry is answered and transformed into structured customer intelligence.

## Phase 2
### Offline store data capture + online retargeting layer
Goal: help VIVAIA UK capture richer customer data from offline store interactions, understand those customers better, and retarget them online.

### What Phase 2 includes
- offline customer data capture from store visits and in-store interactions
- unified CRM profiles combining call data, store data, and customer interests
- consent-based follow-up channels such as WhatsApp or email
- remarketing based on CRM tags, visit behavior, and customer intent
- audience building by category, fit need, and purchase intent
- product recommendations based on enquiry type and in-store interest
- restock alerts, new arrival campaigns, and re-engagement flows
- reporting on offline-to-online conversion behavior

### Example Phase 2 capture points
- in-store QR signups
- assisted shopping or fit consultation capture
- product interest capture by staff
- event or launch RSVP capture
- out-of-stock / size-not-available capture
- try-on interest without purchase

### Example Phase 2 flows
- visited store and showed interest in loafers -> add to loafer audience and retarget online
- asked about fit or comfort -> follow up with best-fit recommendations and related products
- asked about restock -> trigger alert when available
- browsed in store but did not buy -> retarget with relevant styles or category creative
- repeated interest in one category -> build segment for future launches and campaigns

## Strategic logic
This sequencing is stronger because:
1. Phase 1 solves an immediate operational problem.
2. Phase 1 creates the first customer intelligence foundation.
3. Phase 2 expands that foundation into offline store capture, deeper customer understanding, and online retargeting.

## Executive framing
Phase 1 is about answering, capturing, tagging, and analyzing customer enquiries.
Phase 2 is about capturing richer offline store data, understanding VIVAIA UK customers better, and turning store traffic into online remarketing audiences.
